What I did

  1. Designed and manufactured a custom housing using SolidWorks and Cura, optimised for compactness and durability.
  2. Wired and soldered multiple subsystems including drone cameras, lenses, screens and IR floodlights to achieve clear imaging in low light conditions.
  3. Added connectivity to an FPV drone to demonstrate systems integration and forward‑looking application of technology.
  4. Overcame challenges in power management, alignment of optics and ensuring stable electronic connections.
  5. Enhanced soldering and wiring skills as well as troubleshooting under real constraints.
  6. Produced a working prototype capable of real‑time, low‑latency night vision to validate design choices and technical execution.
