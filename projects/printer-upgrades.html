3D Printer Upgrades

  • Klipper
  • Direct drive

1 / 1

What I did

  1. Installed a dual Z axis upgrade for improved stability and accuracy in tall prints and fitted a direct drive extruder for better filament handling and consistency.
  2. Integrated a probe leveller to reduce calibration time and increase first layer reliability.
  3. Replaced the motherboard and migrated firmware to Klipper which is highly customisable.
  4. Implemented Raspberry Pi with PC integration to enable remote monitoring, wireless control and workflow optimisation.
