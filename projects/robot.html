6 DoF Robot and Simulation

Project 04 of 07
  • MATLAB
  • Arduino
  • Inverse kinematics
  • Friction
  • Moulds
  • CAD

What I did

  1. Built and simulated a cable actuated soft surgical manipulator with six degrees of freedom.
  2. Integrated friction‑aware modelling and inverse kinematics into a MATLAB and Arduino pipeline that maps endpoint targets to cable lengths and servo commands.
  3. Fabrication established a repeatable workflow using resin moulds, ejector pins and Ecoflex.
  4. Physical prototyping validated geometry but exposed high internal friction and stick slip, which limited curvature and overloaded servos anded a simulation‑to‑hardware gap.
