Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy explains what personal information is collected when you visit this website and what happens to it. The website is run by Ivan Kossko, based in Scotland, United Kingdom, who is the data controller for the purposes of UK data protection law.

Contact

For any privacy question or request, email kosworksbusiness@gmail.com.

What this website collects

  • No forms or accounts. The site has no sign-up, comment or contact form, so it does not ask you to enter personal details.
  • No analytics or advertising. The site does not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, and does not set cookies.
  • Theme preference. If you use the Theme button, your choice of light or dark is saved in your browser’s local storage (key: prefers-theme) so it is remembered on your next visit. It stays on your device, is not sent to me, and you can clear it any time in your browser settings.
  • Fonts. Fonts are hosted on this site itself, so your browser does not contact Google or other font providers.

Hosting and server logs

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages, provided by GitHub, Inc. When you load a page, GitHub’s servers may process technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver and secure the service. I do not receive or control these logs. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for details.

If you email me

If you contact me by email, I will use your email address and what you tell me only to reply and deal with your message. My lawful basis is legitimate interests (responding to people who contact me), or taking steps you have asked for. I will not sell your details or use them for marketing you did not ask for.

I keep emails for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry and normally no longer than 12 months afterwards, unless I need to keep them longer for a legal reason. My email is provided by Google (Gmail), which processes messages on my behalf.

Links to other websites

This site links to LinkedIn, Instagram and GitHub. Those services have their own privacy policies and may collect data when you visit them. I am not responsible for how they handle your information.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you may have the right to ask for access to your personal information, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable format. To use any of these rights, email kosworksbusiness@gmail.com. I will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how your information has been handled, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO): ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. I would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Changes to this policy

If the way this site works changes, I will update this page and the date above.
